## Deliverables and the exhibition bar

- Something must be made, but scope is flexible for speculative and emerging tech projects.
- **The key bar is exhibition value: work must be presentable in a physical or visual format.** Speculative projects can show the marketing of a future, a visible artefact, or a scenario. Screening films and short-format content may be incorporated.
- This directly supports the Compass launch film as a legitimate deliverable, and strengthens the case for a physical mockup of the glasses to exhibit alongside it.
