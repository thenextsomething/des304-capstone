# World-building for the Compass film

One of the open problems named at the end of DES303: the film only works if it genuinely feels like it came from a specific future. This file holds the decisions as they get made. Everything below is undecided unless marked otherwise.

Known anchors (settled in DES303, see `COMPASS_CONCEPT_AND_STATUS.md`):

- Near-future Aotearoa. The environment stays real and recognisable; the UI is the speculative layer.
- Compass exists to assist, never to create. The world it comes from is one where that distinction matters commercially and culturally.
- Industrial design sits closest to Brilliant Labs Frame: light, minimal, collaborative rather than dominant.

## What year is it?

Not decided. The MUSE game is set in Auckland 2035, which is a candidate for keeping the two artefacts in one shared world. Question to settle: is the film the same world as the game, or adjacent?

## What is the state of Aotearoa in that year?

Not decided. What has changed for daily life, work, and money? What does the AI landscape look like from New Zealand rather than from San Francisco? The brief asks specifically about Aotearoa, and peer feedback said the SF perspective must be actively bridged, not left implicit.

## What has happened to creative work by then?

Not decided. What tools do everyday creators use? What got automated, what resisted? Where does the anxiety sit? This is the emotional backdrop the launch film speaks into.

## Why does Compass exist?

Not decided. Who makes it, what triggered it, what is it a reaction against? An Apple-style launch film implies a company with a point of view. The film may never state any of this, but it has to be decided for the film to feel real.

## Who wears it?

Not decided. Who is the launch aimed at? The draft structure (piano, painting, photography) implies everyday creators rather than professionals. Confirm or change.

## Rules of the world on screen

Not decided. What can the film show and what must it never show? Candidate rules from DES303 experiments: raw unaltered environments, persistent UI that never disappears, suggestions as whispers not instructions.
