# Third direction candidates

The Week 2 capstone presentation needs the strongest direction plus at least two alternatives. Two exist: the Compass launch film (primary) and the MUSE interactive game (secondary). A third has not been identified. This file is the scratchpad for finding it.

## The filter (from the Week 9 crit and Jed)

Any candidate has to survive the questions that killed The Aotearoa Creative:

1. Where does this go?
2. What skill are you actually building?
3. Do you genuinely care about this long term?

Plus Jed's standing rule: the work should connect to something Polo genuinely wants to get better at. Filmmaking passed that test; journalism failed it. Do not revive the journalism direction.

## Where to look

- Adjacent to the professional practice: other speculative film formats (a documentary from the future, a testimonial or unboxing from the Compass world, a customer support or training film) rather than a new medium.
- The physical product itself: the crit said design the object before locking production. A product design direction (the Compass object, packaging, retail presence as diegetic prototypes) would build a real skill and feed the film either way.
- The making-of angle: the prompt intermediary workflow and the AI-assisted production process are themselves an emerging technologies story, though this risks confusing the depicted technology with the making tools, which is against the project's hard rules. Treat with caution.
