## Corrections to numbers already used

**APRA AMCOS 82 per cent.** The A1 deck says creators "feared AI could end their ability to make a living". The published wording is "are concerned that the use of AI in music could lead to music creators no longer being able to make a living from their work". Use the published wording. The 38 per cent already using AI is accurate. Footnote that the sample is APRA AMCOS members across Australia, New Zealand and members living overseas.

**USC LABarometer 59 per cent.** It is Los Angeles County entertainment workers, not the United States generally. Say so.

**Creative New Zealand median income.** $19,500 from creative work alone. The $15,000 figure is specifically Deaf and disabled artists and $16,500 is female creative professionals, so $15,000 is not the headline number.
