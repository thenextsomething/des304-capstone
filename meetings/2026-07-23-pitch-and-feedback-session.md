# Informal concept pitch and feedback (Thursday stream session)

**Date:** Thursday 23 July 2026, 11:20am
**What:** Polo pitched the Compass concept informally to the stream group and got feedback. Left early for a 1:30pm appointment, so missed the afternoon's timeline examples.
**Source:** Granola notes from Polo's recording.

## How Polo pitched it

- Speculative design project on AI and creativity in New Zealand. HMW: use speculative design to help everyday creators feel what it means to work alongside AI, not be replaced by it.
- Core tension: as AI tools improve, does the creative still need to be there? Film, music, video and graphic design communities are all feeling the fear. The project takes the positive framing: AI as collaborator, not replacement.
- The product: AI-enabled AR glasses overlaying information for creatives in real time. Painting example (palettes and mood boards reacting to what is in front of you), piano example (visual cues while learning). Key principle: suggestions are reactive, always based on what the creator is doing.
- References used: an existing AR glasses product for the text overlays, and a speculative humanoid robot launch video as a style reference for the film.

## Feedback received

1. **Clarify the AI angle.** The group asked what specifically the AI is doing in the AR experience. AR and AI collaboration read as different things; the AI's collaborative role has to be explicit in the pitch.
2. **Narrow the scope.** The glasses concept is clear and compelling; the robot reference risks diluting focus. Vision-focused is tighter than broadening into movement or physical assistance.
3. **Deliverable direction endorsed:** a mockup of the glasses plus a live demo-style video showing it functioning. Strong idea; keep the pitch focused on key terms and the problem space.
